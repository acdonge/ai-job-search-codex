**Subject:** Recommendation request for [degree] applications, [entry term/year]

Dear [Title and surname],

I am applying to [type of program] for [term/year], with a focus on [area]. I appreciated
working with you on [shared project/course and dates], where I [specific contribution].

Would you be comfortable writing a strong recommendation based on that work? The
earliest deadline is [verified date/time/timezone]. I have included a short program list
and factual summary, and can provide any other information that would be helpful.
